Cosplayer 守則

服裝與道具

  • 避免過於暴露的服裝或外露內衣，建議穿著安全褲及絲襪。
  • 避免真實軍警或保安制服；請勿攜帶氣槍、煙花、開鋒刀劍或危險硬質道具。

更衣與整潔

  • 請勿因更衣或化妝而阻塞洗手間，亦請勿在內使用噴劑。
  • 請勿散落羽毛、紙碎或閃粉，或使用可能弄污場地的假血。

攝影

  • 拍攝時請保持攤位及主要通道暢通。
  • 請與他人共享攝影位置，避免長時間佔用。

現場協助

  • 請遵從工作人員指示；不安全的服裝或道具可能須作調整，未能配合者可能被要求離場。
  • 請保管個人物品；如遺失物品或需要協助，請向 ACA 工作人員求助。

參考 2025 年守則整理 ↗

Costumes & props

  • Avoid overly revealing costumes or visible underwear. Safety shorts and stockings are recommended.
  • Avoid real military, police or security uniforms. Do not bring airsoft guns, fireworks, sharpened blades or dangerous hard props.

Changing & cleanliness

  • Do not block toilets while changing or applying makeup. Do not use sprays there.
  • Do not scatter feathers, confetti or glitter, or use fake blood that may stain the venue.

Photography

  • Keep booths and main walkways clear when taking photographs.
  • Share photography spaces; do not occupy them for extended periods.

On-site assistance

  • Follow staff instructions. Staff may request changes to unsafe costumes or props, and ask visitors to leave if needed.
  • Look after your belongings. Ask ACA staff for help with lost property or other issues.
