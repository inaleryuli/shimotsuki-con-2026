參加者守則

門票與再次入場

  • 入場後請保留門票，以便檢閱。
  • 參加者可於 18:00 前憑手印再次入場。
  • 現場售票及入場於 18:00 截止。工作人員將於 19:00 起請參加者離場，活動於 20:00 結束。

安全與整潔

  • 場內嚴禁吸煙，請保持場地整潔。
  • 請勿攜帶危險物品進場，例如刀具、玻璃等利器。

通道與舞臺

  • 請勿長時間在同一位置逗留或拍攝，以免造成阻塞。
  • 請按場地指示觀賞表演，切勿過分貼近舞臺，以保障表演者及參加者安全。

個人財物與現場秩序

  • 請保管自身財物及注意人身安全；本會對任何損失不負責任。
  • 違反守則、破壞場地或滋擾他人者，本會有權要求其離場。

現場協助

  • 如有疑問，請向本會工作人員查詢。

守則說明

  • 本會保留以上守則的增修及最終解釋權。
  • 中英文版本如有抵觸或不符，以中文版為準。

官方守則 ↗

Tickets & re-entry

  • Keep your ticket after admission for inspection.
  • Participants may re-enter with a hand stamp before 18:00.
  • On-site ticket sales and admission end at 18:00. Staff begin asking attendees to leave at 19:00. The event ends at 20:00.

Safety & cleanliness

  • Smoking is strictly prohibited. Keep the venue clean.
  • Do not bring dangerous items, including knives, glass or other sharp objects.

Walkways & stage

  • Avoid staying or taking photos in the same place for an extended period if it causes obstruction.
  • Follow venue markings when watching performances. Do not stand too close to the stage, for everyone’s safety.

Belongings & conduct

  • Look after your belongings and personal safety. ACA accepts no liability for losses.
  • ACA may ask anyone who breaches the rules, damages the venue or disturbs others to leave.

Need help?

  • Ask ACA staff if you have any questions.

About these rules

  • ACA reserves the right to amend and interpret these regulations.
  • If the Chinese and English versions differ, the Chinese version prevails.
